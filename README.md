# Age Up Coach – Age of Empires II edition

A coaching overlay for **Age of Empires II: Definitive Edition**. It helps you improve every game.

- **Build orders, step by step.** A tick card follows your villager count and ticks your buildings when you buy them.
- **Alerts.** You get a nudge for an idle Town Center, idle villagers, House soon and Housed.
- **Match tools.** Civ win rates and matchups, counters, and the map tips for the map you're on.
- **Review.** After each game, you get a review of your own recorded game.

It reads only your own screen. It never reads the game's memory or files, never presses keys or clicks for you, and has no tracking.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](../../releases/latest)**: take `AgeUpCoach-AoE2-<version>.zip` under *Assets*.

## Set up (2 minutes)

1. Right-click the zip and choose **Extract All**. The app does not run from inside the zip.
2. Start `AgeUpCoach-AoE2.exe`. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**. The app isn't code-signed yet. Only download it from this page.
3. **Get your free key.** Click *Get a free key*, copy the key, paste it into the app and click *Activate*. A key works for 7 days on one PC. If you already use Age Up Coach for AoE IV, get a second free key for this edition.
4. The setup opens. Find your player name, pick your civ, and check that your game settings match.
5. **In AoE II:** English game language, HUD 100%, 1920×1080, and *Global Queue Items: 2 rows*.
